What you need to do

You used the old site

Your account and completed induction have been moved across, but not your password. To set a new one:

  1. On the new site, select Log In, then Forgot your password?
  2. Enter the email you used on the old site.
  3. Open the link we email you and choose a password.

If you hadn't finished the induction, select Start on your dashboard.

You're new

Select Register, open the verification link we email you, then log in and start the induction.

Questions

Do I have to do the induction again?

No. It was moved across with its original dates. You only do it again when it expires.

Should I register a new account?

Not if you used the old site. Use Forgot your password? with the same email. A new account under a different email won't have your completed induction.

I didn't get the password email.

Check your junk or spam folder, and that you entered the email you used on the old site. Only one email is sent per minute.

My completed induction isn't showing.

Check you logged in with the email you used on the old site. If you finished it there very recently, contact us and we'll check.

What about my old certificate?

Use the new one, under Compliance. It has the same dates, and a QR code anyone can scan to check it.

Can I still use the old site?

No. Anything you do there now won't be recorded.
