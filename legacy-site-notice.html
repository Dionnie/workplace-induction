Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding the platform transition:

Do I have to do the induction again?

No. Your induction records were moved across with their original completion and validity dates intact. You only need to complete the induction again when your current compliance expires.

Should I register a new account?

Not if you used the old site. Use Forgot your password? with the same email you registered previously. Creating a new account under a different email address will not link to your past induction history or certificates.

I didn't receive the password reset email.

Check your junk or spam folders, and verify that you entered the exact email address used on the old site. For system security and rate protection, only one reset email is sent per minute.

My completed induction isn't showing on my dashboard.

Confirm that you logged in with the exact email address used previously. If you completed your induction on the old site immediately before the transition, please contact us using the details below so we can confirm and verify your records.

What about my old certificate?

Use your new certificate located under Compliance. It preserves your original dates and includes a verifiable QR code that supervisors and inspectors can scan directly from their mobile devices.

Can I still complete inductions on the old site?

No. The old site is permanently decommissioned. Any modules completed or submitted there will not be recorded in company compliance records.
